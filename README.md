# Remote Expert Assessment Console

**One line pitch:** A console that lets remote experts review claims, accept genuine errors and reject no crack or no damage claims, protecting margin on every decision.

## Problem
Invalid damage claims were being paid out, eroding margin. Reviewers needed one place to see the evidence and decide fast.

## Users and Jobs to Be Done
Remote experts who need to review a claim, judge the evidence and accept or reject it with a clear reason.

## Solution and Scope
A review queue with claim evidence, one click accept or reject, and reason capture. Left out: payments, customer messaging and automated scoring in v1.

## Key Decisions
- Decision: reviewers reject with a mandatory reason. Why: it creates audit data and lowers disputes. Rejected: free text only. Outcome: add your result here.

## Success Metrics
Primary: margin protected per claim reviewed. Guardrails: review time per claim, dispute rate.

## Built With
JavaScript

## What I'd Do Next
1. Auto flag likely no damage claims. 2. Reviewer accuracy dashboard. 3. Appeals workflow.
